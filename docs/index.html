    How to play

    • You play red and go first. Click or tap a column to drop a disc, or use the arrow keys and Enter.
    • The AI plays white and replies at once, searching ahead with minimax and alpha-beta pruning.
    • Line up four discs across, down or diagonally to win. The winning four light up.
    • Hover over a column to see where your disc will land.
    • Choose Play online to face another person live. Wins and losses change your rating on the leaderboard.

    Play online

    Play another person in real time. Everyone starts with a rating of 1200. Winning raises your rating, losing lowers it, and the ranking is on the Replays and leaderboard page.

    Playing as

    1 to 20 characters. There are no passwords, so pick a name you will reuse.
